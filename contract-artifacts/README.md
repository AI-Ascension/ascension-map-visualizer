# Contract artifacts

Protocol authority stays in `AI-Ascension/sts2-protocol`, consumed as a neutral Rust package at the exact revision in the product Cargo manifest and lockfile. Its legacy gameplay profile remains unchanged.

`harness/` contains inert copies of the independently owned analysis, bundle, and operational-feed JSON schemas. `harness/provenance.json` records, for every copied file, the exact immutable owner commit, source and copy paths, license, and SHA-256 value, together with the copied synthetic fixtures.

The copied files legitimately span two owner revisions, so each entry states its own revision instead of inheriting one repository-wide pin. The three schema copies come from `67e3c241912bf687695bdd9cf547ca53335e5b3c`, the revision that hardened the checkpoint-reference contract those copies validate. The ten synthetic fixture copies come from `3c4470f0940e3668519194e13b59f234e75821a7`, the harness artifact revision recorded in `docs/ASSEMBLY_MANIFEST.json` and the revision compatible with the pinned `sts2-protocol` package. `tools/verify-harness-provenance.mjs` fetches every recorded revision and verifies each copy byte-for-byte against it; `tools/contracts.mjs` rejects a fixture group that disagrees with the recorded artifact revision. The earlier fixture revision is retained by owner branches rather than the owner's default branch, so verification fails closed if that revision is ever pruned; refreshing the record then requires an owner tag or an authorized fixture re-baseline. Never patch a copy without an accepted corresponding owner change and a refreshed provenance record.

Version matching and hashes establish compatibility and content identity. They do not authorize a game action, prove that a producer is deployed, or establish native player-visible scope.
