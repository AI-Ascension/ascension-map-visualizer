# Viewer direction

Visual thesis: a quiet charcoal map workspace with clear original symbols, generous separation between lanes, and a single amber inspection accent.

Content plan: the whole graph is the primary workspace; a compact toolbar holds fit, search, overlays and replay; an adjacent inspector shows the selected node or route; a persistent overview preserves orientation during zoom. Availability, completeness and historical freshness are always visible. This is an operating surface without a marketing hero.

Interaction thesis: smooth fit/zoom transitions preserve spatial continuity; a restrained selection transition connects graph and inspector; replay transitions preserve map identity and respect reduced-motion preferences. Color is supplemented by labels, outlines and symbols.

The graph itself supplies the visual anchor. No proprietary art or decorative image generation is needed. Node clicks inspect only; route selection is annotation and has no mutation authority.
